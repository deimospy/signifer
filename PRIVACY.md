# Signifer privacy policy

**English** · [Español](PRIVACY.es.md)

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Signifer is a QR code and barcode app for Android developed by Luis Roche. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## Summary

Signifer does not collect, send or share any personal data. The app does not have permission to use the internet, so Android itself prevents it from connecting to any server. Everything it does happens on your phone.

## What the app handles and where it stays

- **Camera.** Camera frames are analyzed in memory to find codes and are discarded immediately. They are never saved or sent.
- **Images you choose.** When you read a code from an image, you pick it in the Android system picker. Signifer only receives that image, reads it and does not keep a copy.
- **History.** The codes you scan and create are saved in a database inside the app's private storage on your phone. You can delete entries, empty the history, set how long it is kept or turn it off in the settings. Wi-Fi passwords are not saved unless you ask for it on the result screen.
- **Settings.** Your preferences, such as the language or the formats to read, are saved in the app's private storage.
- **Temporary files.** A logo you add to a code, and an image you share, are kept in the app's temporary folder only while they are needed.
- **Backups.** You can export and import a history backup to a file you choose. Signifer does not send that file anywhere. The app's data is excluded from Android cloud backups and from device-to-device transfers.

Uninstalling Signifer deletes all of its data from your phone.

## Permissions

- **Camera**, to scan codes.
- **Vibration**, for the optional confirmation when a code is read.

Signifer does not request internet, location, contacts, storage or any other permission.

## Sharing with other apps

Signifer never opens anything on its own. When you tap an action, such as opening a website, adding a contact or sharing a code, Android hands that information to the app you choose, and that app's privacy policy applies from then on. Other apps can also ask Signifer to scan a code and receive the result.

## No tracking

Signifer contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking libraries, and it requires no account. The third-party open source libraries it includes run entirely on the device.

## Links

The "Source code" link in the About screen opens GitHub in your browser, where GitHub's own policy applies.

## Children

Signifer is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
